Core Security Pillars

Engineered From First Principles

No Google login, no telemetry beacons, and zero host-page DOM injection.

Zero-Knowledge Sync

100% offline by default. When you opt into cross-device sync, our self-hostable Rust Blind Relay stores only 256-byte padded XChaCha20-Poly1305 blobs and merges updates via Hybrid Logical Clock (HLC) CRDTs.

Built-in 2FA

Generate RFC 6238 6-digit and 8-digit TOTP codes with a live 30-second countdown ring and enroll 2FA QR codes offline straight from your active tab.

WebAuthn & Biometrics

Unlock your vault in milliseconds using hardware-backed WebAuthn PRF on desktop or Secure Enclave / StrongBox on mobile, protected by strict 3-strike fallback.

100% Open Source

Licensed under AGPL-3.0 with deterministic reproducible builds (SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH) so you never have to take our word for it.

Simple 3-Step Workflow

How It Works

Zero cloud signups. Up and running in under 30 seconds.

1

Create Vault

Set your Master Password offline. Arca stretches it via Argon2id (64 MiB) to wrap your 256-bit Data Encryption Key and generates your 24-word recovery phrase.

2

Add Passwords

Save logins, scan 2FA QR codes from your active tab, and autofill safely with our 5-Point Anti-Clickjacking Guard.

3

Sync via Blind Relay

Optionally enable Blind Relay Sync in Settings to synchronize 256-byte padded ciphertexts across desktop and mobile using Ed25519 auth.

Ready to take back control of your credentials?

Available as a Chrome Web Extension and Android mobile app.

Install Arca

Unzip arca-extension-ready.zip and load it in Chrome:

unzip -o arca-extension-ready.zip -d ~/Downloads/arca-extension-ready

Then open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, click Load unpacked, and select ~/Downloads/arca-extension-ready.
