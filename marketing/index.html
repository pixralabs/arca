Architecture & Trust

Frequently Asked Questions

Arca is 100% offline-first. Your vault is created and encrypted locally on your device using Argon2id and XChaCha20-Poly1305 without requiring an email address, cloud account, or central server.
Every vault record is padded via ISO/IEC 7816-4 to fixed 256-byte blocks before encryption, hiding credential lengths. Changes are signed with your device's Ed25519 key and merged conflict-free via Hybrid Logical Clock (HLC) CRDTs.
Upon vault creation, Arca generates a 24-word recovery phrase offline. Because Pixra Labs never holds your keys, your 24-word phrase or encrypted local export is the sole mathematical mechanism to restore your vault.
Yes. Arca is licensed under GNU AGPL-3.0 with deterministic builds (SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH) so you can self-host the lightweight Rust relay in Docker and verify release binaries bit-for-bit.

1. Core Zero-Knowledge Philosophy

All vault records are encrypted locally using Argon2id (64 MiB, t=3, p=4) and XChaCha20-Poly1305 with 256-byte block padding (ISO/IEC 7816-4). Contact: security@pixra.dev.

Account & Data Deletion

Once data or an account is deleted, it is permanently wiped with PRAGMA secure_delete = ON and is mathematically unrecoverable (0-day retention).

Option 1 · Single Vault Item

Delete an Individual Credential

  1. Unlock your Arca vault via Master Password or Biometrics.
  2. Select the credential item you wish to remove.
  3. Tap Delete (Trash icon) and confirm.
Option 2 · Complete Vault Wipe

Delete Entire Account & Relay Blobs

  1. Open Settings → Danger Zone.
  2. Select Delete Account & Reset Vault.
  3. Confirm to destroy local SQLite DB, StrongBox keys, and signed Ed25519 Blind Relay blobs immediately.

Install Arca

Unzip arca-extension-ready.zip and load it in Chrome:

unzip -o arca-extension-ready.zip -d ~/Downloads/arca-extension-ready

Then open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, click Load unpacked, and select ~/Downloads/arca-extension-ready.
